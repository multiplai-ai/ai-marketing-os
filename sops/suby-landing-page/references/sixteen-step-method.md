# Sixteen-step method and page structure

Source: [Sabri Suby, Landing Page Tutorial — 2026 FREE COURSE for Beginners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Env9wuka0oA). Ranges below locate the teaching in directly retrieved English captions; transcription can mishear names and numbers. Operational outputs are our implementation of the teaching. Speaker performance anecdotes are not client forecasts.

## Step 5: the separate seventeen-part selling structure

The following is a paraphrased functional map, not a reproduction of the companion PDF.

1. Identify the intended audience in the eyebrow.
2. Lead with the desired result and a compelling, clear headline.
3. Make the supporting promise more concrete in the subhead.
4. Give the reader a relevant unanswered question.
5. Describe the lived problem and consequences.
6. Introduce the specific solution.
7. Establish credible experience and qualifications.
8. Explain what changes for the buyer, beyond a feature list.
9. Demonstrate the claims with real evidence.
10. Present a compelling offer appropriate to the next decision.
11. Add genuine extras that have independent value, if they exist.
12. Establish why the offer is valuable before discussing cost.
13. Reveal the next-step price/terms after value. For lead generation, this concerns the consultation, not automatically the paid service price.
14. Give a truthful reason to act now; any availability limit must be current and real.
15. Address the buyer's risk with the actual applicable terms.
16. Explain exactly what clicking, booking and attending involve.
17. Close with a P.S. that summarizes value, next action and the practical cost of delay.

Do not force seventeen visual sections. Preserve the persuasion functions. Map each to a concrete page location or explain the omission. When a business has no bonus, deadline or guarantee, propose a responsible option or record the gap; do not fabricate it to fill a row.

## Execution record

Use columns: step; source timestamp; business evidence; decision; artifact/location; status; unresolved action. Include all sixteen steps and all seventeen structure elements. Distinguish implementing a design from completing market tests, interviews, production assets or approvals.
