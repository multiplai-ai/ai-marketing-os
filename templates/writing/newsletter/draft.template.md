# Working subject or title

## Preview opening

Create a sourced reason to keep reading and make the reader promise clear.

## Body arc

Develop one argument or narrative with concrete evidence and natural transitions.

## Resource or action

Give the reader something useful to apply, inspect, or decide.

## Close

End in the author's natural posture and point to the next action only when earned.
