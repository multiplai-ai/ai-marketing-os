# Cedar Books: synthetic Search ad package

US consulting firms; American English; goal: book an introductory call. Evidence is limited to the supplied page snapshot and four synthetic interviews. Keyword priority is an intent-based hypothesis, with no performance data.

Someone searching monthly bookkeeping for consultants wants organized books, worries about what the monthly service includes, and needs to see the package before booking an introductory call.

Lead with package clarity: three of four buyers asked what is included, and section 2 answers that question. Use monthly-close clarity and practical bookkeeping help as supporting angles. These are editorial choices, not performance predictions.
