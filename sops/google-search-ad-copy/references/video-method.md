## Deliberate adaptations

- “Sell the click” means attract a relevant visitor. This skill also checks lead quality and business outcomes; curiosity alone is not success.
- The video uses long illustrative headlines. Convert the underlying idea into independent RSA assets instead of copying examples that exceed the format.
- The 20-headline exercise is an ideation step. It is not permission to put 20 headlines in one RSA.
- A user supplied the actual Halo workbook during skill creation. Its categories, frequency columns, importance score, and glossary are mapped in [halo-grid.md](halo-grid.md). The public package includes the functional text grid, not the original workbook.
- Research should reveal the urgent private question or desired outcome behind the search. Emotional specificity and provocative questions are encouraged when grounded in evidence. Exclude fabricated threats, deceptive secret-reveal promises and unsupported market assumptions; evaluate actual assets against current platform policy.
- The video's universal performance percentages, bidding prescriptions, impression-share targets, budget splits, and statements about Google representatives are not adopted as facts or account-management rules.
- Competitor keyword targeting, trademark use in ad text, and comparative claims have different rules. Do not infer legal clearance from the video; inspect relevant current guidance when that work is requested.

The six-cycle deep scan and the mandatory insight-to-ad bridge are later skill adaptations. See [deep-halo-research.md](deep-halo-research.md) for research-led examples and coverage requirements.

## What the creative method must preserve

The category query names the service; the buyer's private question supplies
its emotional meaning. The skill must carry that question into a selected
headline or description, then make the next click useful. A list of service
features with a research appendix does not implement this method.

The video's provocative examples illustrate that distinction. They do not
establish universal demographics, the prevalence of a particular fear, or
permission to advertise an answer absent from the destination. Research the
current audience's own stakes and language. See the fictional worked examples
in [deep-halo-research.md](deep-halo-research.md) for the difference between a
specific emotional hook and an invented service promise.
