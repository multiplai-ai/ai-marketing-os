## Deliberate adaptations

- “Sell the click” means attract a relevant visitor. This skill also checks lead quality and business outcomes; curiosity alone is not success.
- The video uses long illustrative headlines. Convert the underlying idea into independent RSA assets instead of copying examples that exceed the format.
- The 20-headline exercise is an ideation step. It is not permission to put 20 headlines in one RSA.
- A user supplied the actual Halo workbook during skill creation. Its categories, frequency columns, importance score, and glossary are mapped in [halo-grid.md](halo-grid.md). The public package includes the functional text grid, not the original workbook.
- Research should reveal uncertainty the page can resolve. Sensational claims, vague secret-reveal hooks, negative-life-event pressure, and unsupported market assumptions are excluded.
- The video's universal performance percentages, bidding prescriptions, impression-share targets, budget splits, and statements about Google representatives are not adopted as facts or account-management rules.
- Competitor keyword targeting, trademark use in ad text, and comparative claims have different rules. Do not infer legal clearance from the video; inspect relevant current guidance when that work is requested.

## Example of applying the principle

Hypothetical brief: a bookkeeping service's page explains monthly close steps, transparent packages, and an introductory call. The customer research supplied with the brief says owners worry about disorganized books and not knowing what is included.

| Weak direction | Stronger candidate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Best Bookkeeping Solutions | Know What's in Your Package | Addresses a concrete pre-purchase question if packages are explained on-page. |
| Secret Accountants Hide | See Our Monthly Close Process | Promises useful information the stated page contains. |
| Save 10 Hours Every Week | Get Help With Monthly Books | Keeps an unsupported time-saving number out of the copy. |

These are illustrative candidate assets, not a complete RSA or claims for any real client. Pair benefit assets with service relevance and validate final combinations.
