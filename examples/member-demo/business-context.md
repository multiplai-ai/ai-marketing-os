# Riverton Workshop — fictional business packet

Everything in this packet is fictional demonstration data.

[S1] Riverton Workshop sells scheduling software to independent bicycle repair shops. The buyer is a shop owner who also works at the repair bench. It shows each repair's status and next promised pickup time.
[S2] Supported product facts: a shared repair board, customer pickup reminders, and manual status updates. It has no inventory integration. Pricing has not been decided. No measured time savings, customer totals, quotes, or market rankings are available.
[S3] Desired reader action: request a 15-minute product walkthrough. Voice: calm, practical, use concrete shop examples. Do not invent first-person experience or promise financial results.
[S4] Proposed positioning hypothesis: keep pickup commitments visible while the owner is repairing bikes. This is a hypothesis to test with customers, not validated market research.
[S5] Editorial goal: help an owner decide which repair details the team should record. Keyword: bicycle repair scheduling. Offline only: no live SERP data or external browsing authorized. A supplied competitor excerpt mentions calendars but provides no measured results or ranking evidence.
