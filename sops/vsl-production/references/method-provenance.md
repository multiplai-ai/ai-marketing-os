# VSL method provenance

The original method brief selected these public videos:

- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zv6SD4EVSHk
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lm_PEN6PH-M
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zHeRryZ8X50

They supplied the initial research, angle-testing, teaching, objection and
qualification rubric. This package contains original workflow instructions and
code, not copied captions. Each run must retrieve its selected videos, preserve
timestamped requirements and distinguish editorial guidance from empirical
claims. One or more selected videos are supported; these three are a starting
reference set, not a required source count or permission to infer their contents.

The workflow was promoted from an operating-repository pilot at the owner's
explicit direction. Shared ownership is that owner decision; this migration
does not assert demonstrated adoption by two independent consumers. Business
artifacts, source excerpts and the authored slide deck remain consumer-owned.

The offline evaluation fixtures use invented businesses and sources. They can
demonstrate tool behavior and the agent's recorded source handling, but do not
prove conversion performance, independent review, or signed-release adoption.
