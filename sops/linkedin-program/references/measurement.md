# Measurement and experiments

Review what the program is producing for the selected business. Record the
period, account, source/capture date, publishing volume, and any missing data.
Separate planned posts from actually published posts and report the observed
mix using each post's single primary purpose.

Use the available evidence at four levels:

- Distribution: impressions, profile visits, relevant follower signals.
- Interaction: substantive conversations, returning participants, useful referrals.
- Owned relationships: verified subscriptions, resource use, event attendance.
- Business: qualified conversations, opportunities, wins, and relevant partnership
  or speaking outcomes, with attribution limits.

Do not treat missing metrics as zero, follower count as buyer fit, or registration
as a qualified opportunity. Define qualification from the consumer's ICP and
business process. Preserve denominators when comparing rates and distinguish
unique people from events. Avoid joining identities across tools without reliable
matching and appropriate access. Record direct attribution, self-report, inferred
contribution, and unknown separately; do not imply causality from timing alone.

Review results by purpose, topic, buyer, format, and CTA only where the sample
supports it. Growth and Conversion have different jobs; raw reach alone cannot
choose the better offer. Note production effort as well as outcomes. Explain
what is observed, what might explain it, and what evidence would change the view.

Choose one useful next experiment. For example, compare a broad business outcome
with a narrower specialist message using comparable offers, promotion, audience,
and observation windows. Define the primary decision metric in advance (such as
qualified booked conversations), with registrations/attendance as diagnostics.
Two sequential events are directional evidence, not a controlled A/B test:
seasonality, promotion, sample size, and follow-up differences may explain changes.
Do not declare a winner when the evidence is unavailable or too weak.

Return a concise Continue / Change / Test recommendation tied to evidence, the
proposed change, owner, and next review window. Changing profile copy, offers,
cadence, spend, or distribution remains an explicit operating decision. No
predicted number of leads, guaranteed timeline, or conversion lift is implied.
