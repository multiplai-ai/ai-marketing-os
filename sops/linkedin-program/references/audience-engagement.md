# Audience rooms and engagement

Use three rooms to distinguish why an account is relevant. Rooms describe a
relationship purpose; they are not three ICPs or automatic outreach lists.

| Room | Inclusion reason | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target accounts | Fits an approved customer profile and has a relevant buyer or need | Company fit, buyer role, problem evidence or explicit unknown, source/date |
| Industry voices and publishers | Brings together people the brand wants to reach or learn from | Relevant audience/topic overlap for creators, publications, communities, conferences, organizers, and referral peers |
| Competitors | Helps understand buyers' alternatives and market positioning | Offer/audience overlap and a specific learning question |

Keep company qualification separate from person-level selection. Multiple ICPs
can live within the target-account room. Preserve multiple room labels when
justified but deduplicate the same company/person. A competitor is not a buyer
merely because its content is useful. A publisher's large audience is not proof
that the audience contains the selected buyers.

Turn the brand's choices into explicit filters and exclusion reasons. Geography,
company stage, buyer role, sector, capacity, and buying signals come from its
context; unspecified size or funding ranges remain open. Do not infer purchase
intent from a profile view or a like. Record observed fact versus hypothesis.
For list-building tools, first verify the selected workspace and capabilities;
account creation, enrichment credits, list writes, and exports are separate
operations from defining inclusion rules.

Prepare engagement from current actual posts and relationship history. Prioritize
buyer relevance, a substantive contribution, and opportunities for reciprocal
conversation. Draft a useful addition, specific question, example, or respectful
counterpoint. Do not claim firsthand use or experience without author evidence,
copy a creator's voice, or manufacture familiarity from a weak signal.

Return the post/source link, room, relevance rationale, proposed contribution,
known relationship, and next owner. If posts are unavailable, return priorities
and research gaps instead of fictional comments. Quality and available time set
the workload; no fixed daily comment or connection quota is inherent to this
method. Repeated interaction is context, not an automated permission to DM.

Keep publishing, comments, connections, and inbox ownership explicit. A provider
may own only some lanes. Hand warm conversations to the selected owner with
context and an unresolved next step; never mark a message sent because it was
drafted. Speaking, referrals, partnerships, and useful learning are legitimate
outcomes alongside prospective sales. Use the existing relationship system and
keep personal/contact data in the consumer's approved private storage.
