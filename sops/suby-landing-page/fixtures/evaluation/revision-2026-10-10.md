For independent studio owners

**Know what your cash report is actually telling you.**

The report arrived. You can see the balance. But could you explain what is included in that number?

Bring it to a free 20-minute Cash Report Walkthrough. We will look at it together, locate the balance and identify the questions to take to your accountant.

**Show me how to read my report →**

Bring your existing report. No purchase is required.

**A report should answer a question, not leave you rebuilding it on Sunday.**

If you find yourself going back through the figures to work out what the number includes, another report alone may not solve the problem. Start with the explanation you need.

**The Balance-to-Question Walkthrough connects the number to your next question.**

First we locate the balance. Then we look at what the report says it includes. Finally, we identify what remains unclear and what to ask your accountant. This is a discussion of your report, not tax advice or a promise to change your cash balance.

**Leave knowing what to ask—not wondering what you were sold.**

You get a discussion of your own report and the chance to resolve questions about how the reporting works. There is no written deliverable. Ledger Cedar's monthly reconciliation and cash reporting are separate services; we discuss those only if you ask.

**Twenty minutes. Your report. No fee.**

Choose a time, enter your name and email, and look for the confirmation email. Bring the report you want to understand.

**Will this tell me how much cash I can spend?**

The walkthrough explains the report and identifies questions for your accountant. It does not certify available spending money or guarantee savings.

**Do I have to buy bookkeeping?**

No. The walkthrough is useful on its own. The paid service is a separate decision.

**What should I bring?**

Your existing report and the number or label you find unclear.

**Show me how to read my report →**

P.S. If the next report would leave you with the same question, bring the one you already have. Twenty minutes spent understanding it is the purpose of this free walkthrough.
