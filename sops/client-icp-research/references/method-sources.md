# Method sources and limits

This five-step framework was developed in an operating-repository prototype
on October 5, 2026, following a research and teaching draft on October 2. The
owner requested its promotion to this shared library on October 5. Its decisions
are: find good fits, understand the purchase, choose a group, find real prospects,
and test/use the result. This is an original synthesis, not a named or empirically
validated five-step method from a creator. Use in two active consumers has not
yet been established. The earlier local version is provenance, not a second
shared source to maintain. Shared source is canonical here; consumer-specific
retrieval/storage integrations remain in the consumer.

These sources informed the framework. They are methodological background,
not evidence about the client being researched. Read relevant current originals
when relying on their details; a new client run must gather its own evidence.
No transcript downloads or client research belong in this skill package.

The episode's fictional agency, 10–40 employee filter, ten candidate accounts,
and five conversations were illustrative choices. Do not import any of them as
facts or mandatory thresholds for a real client. Anti-ICP means incompatibility
with the particular offer; it does not mean everyone outside one example's filters.
