# Page and evaluation deliverable

Create a usable commercial draft and its implementation, scaled to the request. The visitor should see persuasion; the team should see evidence and outstanding decisions in separate notes.

## Before copy

Record audience, awareness, incoming promise, wanted business outcome, specific immediate offer, paid-service boundary, actual owner-pitch source, Halo source, proof assets and conversion destination. Compare candidate angles and state why the draft uses one. A choice without test results remains a hypothesis.

## Copy and design

Map the seventeen selling functions from [the method](sixteen-step-method.md) to the actual page in reading order. Deliver the finished headline complex, lead-in, solution and named mechanism, benefits, proof, next-step offer, objections, CTA explanation and P.S. Record absent commercial elements separately rather than fabricating them.

Write down the concrete value the visitor receives from continuing, watching or booking. Check the food-court test before layout. Build the user's requested funnel with consistent offers and truthful transitions. Include desktop and mobile reading order and actual implementation where requested. Use permitted work samples and distinguish craft demonstrations from outcome evidence.

## Evidence and execution

Maintain a compact claim ledger: exact wording; business source; evidence type; permission/currentness; what it proves; limitation. Maintain all sixteen method steps with status and output pointers. Attach source links, not private raw records, to shared design notes.

## Evaluation and handoff

Run [the evaluation](evaluation.md) on the exact copy and rendered artifact. Save specific findings, changes and recheck results. Report open failures rather than a blanket pass. Separate business signoff, copy/design assessment, technical readiness and market results.

For a design file, video playback, form delivery, calendar behavior, speed and analytics remain runtime untested until built. For market validation, specify angle/offer controls, lead-source tracking, qualification and downstream customer measures; do not claim an experiment ran when only a plan exists.
