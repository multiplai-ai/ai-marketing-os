# Fieldnote Desk — fictional source packet

All businesses, roles, quotes, and measurements in this packet are synthetic evaluation data. These are supplied inputs, not market research results or real customer records. Work offline from this packet.

## S1 — Owner intake, 2026-09-10

Fieldnote Desk is an early paid B2B product for independent residential inspection firms with 2–8 inspectors. It helps an office coordinator request missing job photos and file links from inspectors, attach responses to the correct job, and see which packets are ready for the owner to review before invoicing. It does not perform inspections, certify compliance, create inspection reports, send invoices, or integrate with accounting software. It imports job references from a CSV and accepts manually pasted links. Current production features are shared job checklists, reminder drafts, and a missing-evidence view. Automated sending is not available.

We have 6 paying firms, all referred by the founder. Over the next 90 days we want 4 more paying firms of similar size. We can onboard at most 2 new firms in a month. We charge $120 per firm per month with no long-term contract. The same price applies to current and new firms. A larger market estimate has not been researched.

Today the coordinator chases links in text messages and maintains a spreadsheet, usually on Friday before invoicing. The owner reviews the packet and makes the buying decision; an inspector submits requested links and uses the checklist. The trigger is completed site work with missing attachments. The founder thinks coordinator time and fewer missing attachments are promising message angles, but no primary positioning anchor or message has been adopted. Some firms might outsource admin; we have no usage data for that alternative or any competitor performance research.

## S3 — Pilot observation, 2026-09-06

One coordinator self-reported 70 minutes collecting links for one 12-job Friday packet before the pilot, and 45 minutes for a different 12-job Friday packet while using Fieldnote Desk. These were two different weeks, not a controlled trial. Neither invoice time nor revenue was measured. No retention or conversion-rate data is available. The observation cannot establish an average or a causal effect across firms.
