# Method background

Prepared October 4, 2026 from the original offer research. This is a portable
method summary, not fresh research about a new business or proof of conversion.
The live research protocol still runs for each new offer. These are practitioner
heuristics; do not adopt promotional results, historical prices or blanket legal
claims as facts about the user's business.

- [Sabri Suby, supplied Godfather-offer video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OE4u-PFYSns): original research read 807 caption segments, roughly 25 minutes. Buyer demand/features-to-benefits (01:31–06:41); ideal result constrained by delivery (09:15–11:20); rationale (12:53); value (14:26); price (15:28); premiums (16:32); guarantee (17:34); genuine scarcity (20:07). Automatic captions have errors. Six named components are identifiable despite a spoken reference to seven; do not invent a seventh. The suggested tenth-of-value pricing is not a default.
- [Hormozi, Value Equation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=joF2fMj3XKk): original research read the short transcript. Increase outcome relevance and confidence; reduce delay and effort. This is a design lens, not a numeric valuation formula.
- [Stiving interview with Blair Enns](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ep27-blair-enns-pricing-creativity-applied-products-stiving-ph-d-): objectives, measures, value and willingness to pay precede prescribing; compare meaningful alternatives. Published July 22, 2019; transcript imperfections possible.
- [Stark on subscriptions and retainers](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jonathanstark_how-does-the-subscription-model-differ-from-activity-7096226340290342913-AzfH): distinguish advisory access from recurring production. Terminology varies.
- [Stark sample retainer proposal](https://jonathanstark.com/dl/Retainer-Proposal-for-BigCo.pdf): response timing, assumptions and exit/payment terms make a retainer concrete. Historical sample prices are not recommendations.
- [Win Without Pitching, being too expensive](https://www.winwithoutpitching.com/insights/coaches-corner-the-solution-for-being-too-expensive): a bounded strategy-phase remedy illustrates risk reduction. A practice anecdote is not causal evidence.
- [Hormozi on prepayment](https://x.com/AlexHormozi/status/1795083625634963940): terms, bonuses and guarantees can be designed together; do not default to discounts.
- [Sanchez on productized services](https://x.com/Codie_Sanchez/status/1714637947574857897): distinguish unlimited requests from one active request. The secondary case's historical revenue/pricing claims are not adopted.

Professional-services adaptation: a project ends at acceptance; an advisory
retainer supports repeated decisions; an execution retainer supplies bounded
recurring throughput. Price for buyer value and check delivery feasibility.
A guarantee is optional. A diagnostic must stand alone. Missing evidence remains a gap.
