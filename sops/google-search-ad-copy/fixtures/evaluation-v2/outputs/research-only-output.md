Research remains **partial**: these are 14 fictional first-person accounts in four supplied source groups, not verified customer testimony or a completed live Halo scan. The fixture's original request for ads does not override your research-only instruction.

Before writing copy, the most useful additions would be:

1. **Recent original interviews or support/cancellation records**, with dates and buyer context, to test whether these synthetic patterns occur among Harbor's intended customers. A full live scan normally aims for 20 relevant distinct accounts across four source families, but adding six invented or irrelevant accounts would resolve nothing.
2. **Positive and contrary examples**, especially successful internal teams and satisfied outsourcing buyers, to identify who benefits and when.
3. **Harbor's approved process details and proof**: request ownership, status verification, handovers, spending approvals and escalation. Buyer complaints cannot establish that Harbor prevents those failures.
4. **Exact landing-page wording and buyer-fit context**: portfolio size, geography, coverage hours, who decides, common search questions and any approved voice constraints. The supplied snapshot supports only its stated services.

Useful language to preserve in later research includes “owner meeting” (I2: accountability setting), “own the next phone call” (I4: responsibility rather than software), and “handovers” (F2: transfer of request context).

Could you supply recent sales or cancellation notes and the handover/status process details, or point me to where those records live?
