## Coherence and practical limits

Product copy does not add automatic sending, accounting integration, report creation or certification. The owner remains the buyer, and inspector adoption is a concern to investigate rather than an interviewed conclusion.

The content draft preserves the business goal of four additional paid firms in 90 days and onboarding cap of two monthly. It explicitly makes research compete with content time within four weekly marketing hours; the advisor's 20 minutes every other week is not an extra weekly program. The proposed $150 monthly spending limit does not imply a new tool purchase. Demo/onboarding capacity beyond the stated cap needs operational confirmation before setting dates.
