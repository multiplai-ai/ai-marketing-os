# Blog/article editorial brief

## Audience and promised outcome

Define the specific reader, what they should understand or do afterward, and the conversion posture.

## Angle and argument

State one thesis, its tension, the strongest counterargument, and what the piece will not cover.

## Required source and evidence

List firsthand evidence, verified claims, citations, exact phrases, sensitive details, and unresolved claims.

## Structure and depth

Describe the opening pressure, logical section arc, mechanisms, examples, implications, and action section. Set a useful depth target rather than padding to a word count.

## Search and discovery

Name the reader question and search language only when supported by the assignment. Never force keywords into source language.

## CTA and supporting assets

Define the primary action, destination, offer, visual or downloadable support, and required proof.

## Review gates

Require source fidelity, claim resolution, human-writing review, voice/exemplar review, sensitivity review, and message match with the destination.
