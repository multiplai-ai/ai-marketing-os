# Normal case: fictional booking review

Create a teaching VSL for a fictional appointment-based business. The proposed
service reviews how an enquiry becomes a booking, delivers a process map and
prioritized test plan, and leaves implementation with the buyer. The CTA is an
application followed by a written scope. Use an invented speaker without a
biography or customer-result claim. Keep prices out because terms are unapproved.

The JSON files under `inputs/` are synthetic agent-authored stage artifacts.
Captions and research quotes are invented test data, not observed public sources.
The desired output is a complete recording draft with those holds still visible.
Acceptance: no structural defects, evidence holds preserved, exact section-to-shot
coverage, and no owner approval or market-validation claim.
