# Social post type

## Purpose and use condition

Describe the job this post type performs and the evidence required before it may be used.

## Evidence references

- Taste: approved examples that represent editorial preference.
- Owned performance: first-party results from the author's own posts.
- Category performance: broader market evidence, kept distinct from owned results.

## Source requirements

Name the required long-form handoff fields, claim support, exact phrases, and destination readiness.

## Structure, length, and rhythm

Describe useful tendencies, not a mandatory fill-in-the-blanks formula.

## Media and CTA posture

Define when media helps and how the desired action relates to the destination.

## Positive guidance and anti-patterns

Explain what successful examples do. Record failure modes without reducing voice to banned words.

## Draft template

Provide a loose arrangement of ideas that may be changed when the source requires it.

## Performance hypothesis

State what audience behavior this type is expected to influence and what evidence would update that belief.
