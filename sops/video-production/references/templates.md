# Scene Templates

Read when planning reusable layouts. These are structural options, not approved
visuals or a mandatory sequence. Apply the resolved consumer identity.

| Scene | Job | Main inputs |
|---|---|---|
| Opening title | Identify the video and establish its visual direction | Title, approved logo, duration |
| Section card | Orient the viewer at a meaningful transition | Short heading, transition timing |
| Presenter lower third | Identify a speaker without hiding footage | Name, role, duration, safe area |
| List or framework | Make the spoken structure visible | Labels, order, readable density |
| Quote | Attribute a verified quotation | Exact text, source, attribution |
| Comparison | Explain a real difference | Supported claims, balanced layout |
| Product inset | Show a relevant interface action | Actual screenshot/video, crop, timing |
| CTA | Make the next step clear | Approved message and destination |

Choose the minimum scene set that supports the edit. Full-screen backgrounds and
transparent overlays have different delivery requirements. Preview with actual footage.
