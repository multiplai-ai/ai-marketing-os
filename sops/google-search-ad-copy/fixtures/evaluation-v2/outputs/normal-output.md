## Strategy

Primary non-branded, transactional theme: **property maintenance coordination**, for US residential property managers in English. Discovery-call bookings are the conversion. Theme priority is an intent-based hypothesis: no spend, search-term or qualified-conversion evidence was supplied. Keep branded, competitor and informational intent separate.

Someone searching property maintenance coordination wants someone to coordinate the next action, worries that they will still have to chase repairs and explain failures to owners, and needs to understand who coordinates contractors, how status is tracked, and how requests survive a handover.

Lead with the owner-meeting question, supported by the repeated chase and handover angles. I2 explicitly describes embarrassment after reporting a repair fixed when it was not; C3 and R1 independently support the importance of answering owners accurately. This is high emotional intensity and high decision relevance, with three accounts supporting the broader owner-status theme. It does not establish that all managers fear losing their jobs. Confidence is useful within this synthetic packet, low for market generalization. Repeated follow-up has broader recurrence but less uniform explicit emotional depth. Handover has two independent accounts and a particularly close destination match.

## Editorial and verification result

- **Recognition: pass within the fixture.** H1 evokes I2's owner meeting, H2 I1's repeated plumber call, and H4 C2/F2's handover. These scenes are visible in selected assets.
- **Emotional fidelity: pass.** Credibility, chasing frustration and repeated-story frustration survive in the leads; no invented job-loss threat or sanitized generic benefit replaces them.
- **Substitution: pass for the selected leads.** The owner meeting, repeated plumber call and coordinator handover would not fit an unrelated buying situation unchanged. They retain the specific problem and stakes behind this search. This tests research dependence, not exclusive wording or a unique product advantage; a direct category competitor can share a buyer situation. Brand/service lines remain generic support.
- **Click value: pass against supplied snapshot.** Coordination, tracking and handover answer the lead questions. No secret, emergency promise or unavailable download is advertised.
- **Specificity without fiction: pass.** All circumstances trace to fixture IDs. No invented number, testimonial, credential, guarantee or market prevalence claim appears in selected assets.
- **Range and combinations: pass with proposed pins.** Three lead scenes, desired owner clarity and spending control supply range. A whole RSA of accusatory questions is avoided; declarative/process assets and descriptions provide answers.

What's Open?”** I2 changed the focus from a generic quality claim to the specific moment when wrong status damages perceived competence; R1 supports the positive goal of being able to answer.
