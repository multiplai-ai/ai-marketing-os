# Distribution and provenance

AI Marketing OS is distributed under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
On September 13, 2026, MultiplAI's owner confirmed ownership or permission to
publish the inherited procedures and code and selected MIT for the public code.

The first public snapshot omitted six inherited GEO adaptations. A separately
owned, standalone GEO Audit and its local page-audit tool were added in
`v4.0.0-rc.15`. On September 15, 2026, the owner directed inclusion of the five
remaining GEO procedures and supporting tools. Their operational detail is
preserved, with obsolete private references replaced by a local method guide.
The inherited third-party reference corpus is still excluded.
See the [review record](docs/third-party-review.md).

`provenance.yaml` and individual SOP metadata retain source lineage. Historical
source commits may be private; they are not required to install or use this
public release. Dependencies are installed separately and retain their own
licenses. Referenced articles, product names, and external services retain their
respective owners' rights. No API access, subscription, or third-party content
rights are bundled with this code.

The Google Search Ad Copy workflow adapts ideas described in Sabri Suby's
video, "Give Me 24 Minutes and I'll Make You Disgustingly Good at Google Ads,"
and the functional structure of its Halo research worksheet. The workflow's
source notes identify the video and distinguish adaptations from source claims.
The original video, transcript, and Excel worksheet are not bundled or relicensed.
