# Fieldnote Desk: provisional positioning worksheet

A supportable starting sentence is: **Fieldnote Desk is a shared checklist for seeing which photos and links are missing from a job.** This describes the supplied product. It does not establish who needs it, why they would buy or what it replaces.

## JTBD and alternatives to investigate

The partial job is identifying missing job material. Manual messages/folders, an adapted general task tool, outsourced collection, specialized software and simply leaving the material incomplete are possible categories to investigate. None is a reported current alternative in this packet. Their prevalence, limitations and costs are unknown. Do not position as superior to a named competitor or claim a saving.

A primary anchor cannot yet be justified. Conditional direction: if a user currently searches separate messages for job attachments, a checklist may offer a visible record of what remains missing. If the real difficulty is gathering files rather than knowing what is missing, visibility alone may not solve their problem. These are contrasting hypotheses to test, not customer findings.
