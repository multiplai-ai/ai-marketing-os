**Recommended main message: See what’s missing before the owner reviews the job.**

Fieldnote Desk is a shared checklist for inspection offices collecting job photos and file links. The message keeps the provisional text-thread/spreadsheet anchor from positioning and the coordinator-first entry point from ICP. It identifies an actual activity instead of promising general efficiency.

Two supporting arguments:

1. **A job-based view of missing attachments.** Shared checklists and the missing-evidence view support this capability claim (S1).
2. **Keep responses with the job they belong to.** Job references can be imported from CSV and responses associated with the correct job; links are manually pasted (S1). Do not imply automatic capture or accounting integration.

Friend explanation: “It helps an inspection office collect the photos and links for each job and see what’s still missing.” That is proposed copy, not a user quote. The message is specific to this workflow; exclusivity versus every competitor has not been established.

## Voice usable in drafts

Practical, calm and concrete, as S4 requests. Use the language of jobs, links, photos, checklists and review. Explain each manual step. Avoid sweeping statements about the entire inspection industry.

| We could say — proposed copy | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| See which photos and links are missing for this job. | Eliminate inspection chaos forever. | Describes capability instead of a guarantee. |
| Import your job references from a CSV. | Seamlessly integrate your entire operation. | Exact supported input method. |
| Review a sample packet in a demo. | Join the firms saving half their admin time. | No validated saving or broad benchmark. |
| Keep the response with the job. | Let our AI do the inspection for you. | No inspection execution or AI claim is supplied. |

Start by listing what’s missing for each job.” This is original proposed wording, not an interview quote.
