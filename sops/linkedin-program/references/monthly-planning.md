# Monthly planning

Start with the available sources and realistic capacity. Separate content
purpose (why a post exists), topic (what it covers), buyer (who it helps), and
format (how it appears). A video is a format, not a fifth purpose.

## Purpose mix

If the brand has no adopted mix, propose the following as a starting hypothesis,
not a performance benchmark or a required publishing quota:

| Primary purpose | Starting share | Reader job | Suitable source material |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Growth | 40% | Notice a relevant issue or perspective | Verified developments, a defensible point of view, a useful interpretation of a familiar problem |
| Authority | 30% | Trust the author's judgment and approach | Actual frameworks, worked examples, supported case studies, practical decisions |
| Conversion | 20% | Take an available next step | A useful resource, subscription, event, relevant offer or conversation |
| Personal | 10% | Understand the person and their values | Actual experiences, choices, lessons, relevant community participation |

Choose one primary purpose before drafting. A case study with a newsletter
link may remain Authority with a secondary Conversion label. Count it once.
Broad Growth material should still attract relevant buyers; popularity alone
is not the objective. Match explanation depth to the buyer's decisions: enough
detail to be useful, without turning every post into an implementation tutorial.
Technical detail is appropriate when that buyer needs it.

Work from actual publishable slots in the chosen period, not an assumed
four-week month or five-day cadence. If cadence is unknown, return a clearly
labeled scenario and ask for capacity before assigning real dates. Convert the
chosen percentages into whole counts: calculate shares, take their floors,
then assign remaining slots by largest fractional remainder. For ties, use
Growth, Authority, Conversion, Personal order unless the owner selects another
priority. Show both the target and actual resulting shares. Examples at the
starting mix: 20 slots → 8/6/4/2; 12 slots → 5/4/2/1. Small plans cannot exactly
match all proportions; explain rounding rather than filling them with weak posts.

## Assemble and sequence

Read existing ideas and recent publications; keep original IDs and sources.
Sources may include author interviews, real work, approved articles, recordings,
reading, buyer questions, and event observations. Ask for missing substance,
not another interview when usable author input already exists. An AI summary
is not interchangeable with original words when attributing an experience.

Select distinct ideas across the purposes and buyers. Make the method visible
through evidence and judgment, not claims that no competitor can do it. Keep
some movable slots for timely material within the capacity total. When a timely
post displaces another, record the change and recalculate the actual mix.

For each slot include ID, date or relative slot, buyer, primary/secondary
purpose, topic/angle, source, proposed format, takeaway, CTA and destination
readiness, owner, and unresolved production needs. Empty evidence is a gap,
not permission to invent a client story, statistic, quote, or available offer.

Use a rolling detailed preview when useful; later dates may remain brief-level.
Respect existing campaigns and review windows. Do not impose weekday formulas.
If weekly clips and alternating personal posts compete for the same slot,
place clips in other suitable slots or revise the proposal with the owner;
do not silently lose the clip commitment or increase total volume.

## Production handoff

Send the selected brief and source packet to `linkedin-post` or the consumer's
existing writing coordinator. Extract different arguments from a longer source;
do not create five paraphrases of its thesis. For clips, use the actual recording
and timecodes. A script alone cannot establish a recorded quote. Start near the
substance, with enough context to preserve meaning; duration and orientation
follow the material, destination, and selected brand recipe, not a universal rule.

Before proposing Conversion copy, inspect whether the resource, signup, event,
or offer actually exists and matches the promise. If absent, leave the slot held,
propose asset creation, or offer a supported substitute with the count changed.
Do not invent prices, capacity, urgency, or automatic enrollment. Return the plan,
allocation, evidence gaps, and next production actions to the existing system;
no scheduling or publication is implied.
