# Distribution and provenance

AI Marketing OS is distributed under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
On September 13, 2026, MultiplAI's owner confirmed ownership or permission to
publish the inherited procedures and code and selected MIT for the public code.

The first public snapshot omits the six GEO procedures, their adapters,
reference corpus, and associated GEO tools. Those adaptations remain unresolved
and are not covered by this public distribution decision. See the
[review record](docs/third-party-review.md).

`provenance.yaml` and individual SOP metadata retain source lineage. Historical
source commits may be private; they are not required to install or use this
public release. Dependencies are installed separately and retain their own
licenses. Referenced articles, product names, and external services retain their
respective owners' rights. No API access, subscription, or third-party content
rights are bundled with this code.
