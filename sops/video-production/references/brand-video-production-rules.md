# Video Brand QA

Read the current-brand resolution section of [the procedure](../SKILL.md) before styling.

- Match every color, font, logo and component to an approved consumer source.
- Confirm channel-specific rules and variant usage.
- Check actual font loading and licensing; do not substitute silently.
- Inspect text contrast, title safe areas, cropping, motion and overlay transparency.
- Keep source links and approval status with the editable handoff.
