# Newsletter editorial standard

The edition rewards an existing reader relationship rather than imitating a feed post. Its promise, evidence, body arc, and close come from the source packet. It distinguishes verified facts from firsthand observation and unresolved claims. It may use warmth, humor, or asides when supported by the author's voice guide. Run the human-writing gate, then review aloud for source fidelity, natural rhythm, sensitivity, and a CTA that matches its destination.
