# Newsletter editorial brief

## Audience and promised outcome

Define the recurring reader relationship, this edition's promise, and the action it should enable.

## Angle and argument

State the thesis, trigger, tension, counterargument, and what makes this edition timely.

## Required source and evidence

List firsthand evidence, verified claims, citations, exact phrases, sensitive details, and unresolved claims.

## Body arc and depth

Describe the preview opening, narrative or argument arc, useful resource or action section, and close. Avoid a fixed weekly formula when the source calls for a different shape.

## CTA and supporting assets

Define the primary action, offer or resource, destination, and proof the reader needs.

## Review gates

Require source fidelity, claim resolution, human-writing review, voice/exemplar review, sensitivity review, and destination-message-match review.
