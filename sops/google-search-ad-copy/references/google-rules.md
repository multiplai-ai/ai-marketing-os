# Google Search copy checks

Verified 2026-10-03. Refresh official sources when current behavior or policy interpretation affects the work.

From [Google's RSA guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7684791?hl=en): provide 3–15 headlines (30 characters each), 2–4 descriptions (90 each), and up to two optional paths (15 each). Count spaces and punctuation. Double-width characters count twice. Assets can reorder and some may not show; do not rely on three headlines or two descriptions always appearing. Pinning restricts combinations. For required wording, prefer a verified appropriate placement such as headline 1 or description 1; ensure every alternative at that position preserves the requirement.

The validator uses Unicode East Asian W/F width as a practical counting approximation. Non-ASCII text and dynamic insertions require platform verification. It is not a Google rendering engine.

From [Misrepresentation](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6020955?hl=en): avoid deceptive or sensational teasers, pressure based on negative life events, unlikely promised outcomes, hidden material costs, and offers that cannot be found at the destination. Useful specificity is the preferred way to create interest.

Apply [Editorial requirements](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6021546?hl=en): use clear language, conventional capitalization and punctuation, and no gimmicky symbols. The helper flags headline exclamation marks for review; manually inspect repeated punctuation and unnatural capitalization.

For competitor copy, consult [Trademarks](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6118?hl=en) and verify comparative facts. For regulated offers, consult the relevant policy for the vertical and geography. Do not claim Google approval from this checklist.
